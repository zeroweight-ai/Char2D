# Playground runtime

`controller.ts` keeps keyboard input, press/hold/toggle actions, cooldowns, movement locks, jumping and root motion separate from canvas rendering. It reads every published move and both saved binding profiles from the character manifest. `PlaygroundWorld` renders that state with the shared animation timeline, frame scales, foot anchors and sound cues.

Opening `/playground` immediately loads the character list and opens the character picker. “Start playing” enters the single implemented Market Adventure map with the selected hero. There is no overworld selection screen. Canceling leaves a “Choose character” button that reopens the picker; changing characters during a run keeps the current game available if the picker is canceled.

The village uses its original 1672 × 941 coordinates. Standing heroes render at 234 map pixels tall and spawn at (260, 745) on the lower cobblestone road; other poses preserve their saved size relative to Idle. `world.ts` defines the collision geometry in those same image coordinates: solid road foundations, five log bridge surfaces, one-way grassy ledges, and the barrel and stacked crates on the chicken ledge. Background buildings, trees, signs and the fountain are scenery.

The controller uses a stable, foot-anchored body (52 × 234 pixels) so changing animation frames or effects cannot move the collision box. It sweeps movement and action root motion against solid sides, ceilings and platform tops in slices of at most 1/120 second. Ledges allow upward passage and catch descending feet. Walking off an edge starts a fall; grounded state comes from the actual supporting surface, so jumping and crouching work on upper platforms too. Small rises on the bridge are stepped automatically. Touching the spike tips or falling below the map resets the character and releases inputs; walking underneath the spike ledge is safe.

Playground movement applies a 2× multiplier to saved walk speed and a 2.5× multiplier to jump velocity, including their fallbacks. Jump height is limited by headroom at the current platform, keeping the hero inside the map while allowing access to the top ledges. Each character's crouch slowdown and gravity setting still apply. With the standard movement settings, climb from the right road to the mushroom ledge, the chicken ledge, the stacked crates, and the upper grass beside the house. From there jump left to the market, then descend to the garden and left treasure ledges.

The pink ladder (x=1248–1314, y=272–485) connects the chicken and house ledges. In reach of it, hold W/↑ to climb up or S/↓ to climb down; the Climb ↑/↓ buttons also support pointer, touch and Space/Enter holds. Climbing centers the hero on the ladder, suspends gravity and stops at the adjoining ledge. Release to pause, move sideways to let go, or jump to dismount. Jumping requires releasing the climb input before grabbing again. Away from the ladder, vertical keys retain their saved action bindings. The saved `climb` animation is used if available, otherwise Walk while moving and Idle while paused; no new generated assets are required.

`interactions.ts` owns coin pickup and chest combat. The five gold coins near the market are collected by body contact, including during jumps and climbing. Use the character's generated offensive moves through their action buttons or saved keys to strike in the facing direction. Actions such as Attack, Slash, Punch and Fireball, and moves with `attack`/`hit`/`damage` events strike. There is no extra attack button or default F shortcut; F works only when explicitly assigned in the character's saved bindings. Attacks reach 150 map pixels, hit during 60–220 ms of a 280 ms swing, and have a 400 ms minimum cooldown; saved action triggers, cooldowns and movement locks still apply.

The active backdrop is `public/worlds/market-adventure.png`, copied unchanged from “Vibrant Pixel-Art Market Adventure.png”. It preserves the same platform and ladder layout and has no baked-in coins or treasure chests. `render-items.ts` draws all coins, intact chests, rubble and pickup/break effects on a separate canvas, so removing an object reveals the clean background without scenery patches.

Each treasure chest breaks once and spills ten coins that must be touched to collect. The HUD counts collected coins and destroyed chests. Input resets and hazard respawns retain collected coins and broken chests; choosing another character or re-entering the world starts a new run. Attack visuals come from the character's generated animation frames.

After all 25 coins are collected (five on the map and ten from each chest), `GoblinSoldier` enters once from beyond the right edge and walks left at 140 map pixels/second. Its feet follow the same lower-road and log-bridge collision surfaces. It waits at x=180 if the hero has not defeated it, keeping the fight available. Input resets and hero respawns do not restart its entrance or restore its health.

The goblin has three health points. Generated offensive actions use the same facing, reach and hit window as chest attacks, with a body hitbox that excludes the mace. Each swing can hit it only once. A successful hit briefly flashes and pauses the goblin for 300 ms; the third hit immediately removes it and freezes game input and movement. `PlaygroundWorld` opens the accessible modal “You Win!” with one primary button, “Play again”. Replaying keeps the same character and starts a fresh Market Adventure session with new coins, chests and goblin health. A run counter ensures that replaying creates a new controller.

The hero and living goblin also have solid bodies (52 × 234 and 96 × 260 map pixels). Each physics slice sweeps the hero against the goblin's current body, then the goblin against the hero's updated body; walking and action root motion cannot swap their sides or push through a stationary opponent. Vertical separation allows movement on higher platforms and sufficiently high jumps. Falling onto the goblin's head gives the hero a grounded support to jump from. Actor overlap after a respawn is separated into a clear position, and inactive/defeated goblins create no collision shape. The goblin's offscreen entrance uses an extended road surface and the same collision solver.

`public/worlds/goblin-soldier/walk.png` and `walk.json` contain the four-frame, left-facing Muse walk animation generated from `data/assets/map/Pixel Goblin Brute with Spiked Mace.png`. Frames are extracted with the existing chroma removal/native-frame pipeline and drawn at a shared 260-pixel display height on their own canvas. `npm run goblin:generate` reprocesses the cached raw strip in `data/goblin-soldier` without AI calls. If no raw strip exists, it makes one real Muse image request; `--force` explicitly requests a new paid generation, and `--process-only` forbids generation.
